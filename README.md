# Leaba Semiconductor

Leaba Semiconductor was an Israeli fabless semiconductor startup founded in 2014 in Caesarea by Eyal Dagan (CEO) and Ofer Iny (CTO), who had previously sold Dune Networks to Broadcom in 2009. Leaba designed high-end networking silicon — switch and router ASICs for carrier-class packet processing.

Cisco announced its intent to acquire the roughly 45-person company on 2 March 2016 for about $320 million in cash and assumed equity awards plus retention incentives, before Leaba had shipped a product. The Leaba team became the anchor of Cisco's Israel silicon engineering center, and its technology underpins **Cisco Silicon One**, launched in December 2019 as the programmable networking chip family behind the Cisco 8000 Series carrier-class routers.

**Status:** Acquired by Cisco (2016). Successor product: [Cisco Silicon One](https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/products/networking/silicon-one/index.html).

Backed by: bessemer-venture-partners, pitango-venture-capital

## API surface

None. Leaba never operated an independent developer program, public API, SDK, or documentation surface, and the `leaba.com` domain no longer resolves to a website. No API artifacts are applicable to this repo; it is retained as an acquired-company record.
